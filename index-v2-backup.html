What your site is costing you — SuperSmile Orthodontics —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a parent looking for braces for a child, or an adult looking at clear aligners, would. Your phone is a call link on every page, there is a free-consultation request form, and your pages cover every treatment. The leaks are a very heavy homepage, two different sets of opening hours, and thin business details behind the scenes.

Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

The homepage is very heavy and slow in the load test.

I ran a simulated slow-connection load test three times. Your homepage's first content appeared at 6.9, 5.4 and 2.6 seconds. Its largest part, the “Your Smile, Elevated” headline over the video at the top, appeared at 28.0, 9.3 and 23.6 seconds. The page weighed about 16 to 21 MB. In the first run the two hero videos were about 3.8 MB each and the page made 130 requests. A parent comparing orthodontists on a slow connection may not wait for it.

02

Two different sets of opening hours appear on the same pages.

The contact block above the consultation form on the homepage says “Mon–Fri: 7AM–2PM”. The footer, and your Contact page, say “Mon–Fri: 8AM–5PM”. 20 of the 31 pages I opened show both. My accessibility check also found that the 7AM–2PM line is white text on a pale lavender background (contrast 1.5, where 4.5 is the minimum), so it is hard to read.

03

Your business details are thin behind the scenes.

The homepage markup describes an organization and a place with a name and street address. It has no phone number, no opening hours, no practice type such as orthodontist, and no social profile links, though the page itself has all of them. 25 of the 31 pages I opened have no markup at all, including every service page.

04

27 of your 35 blog posts have long titles.

The titles run from 47 to 137 characters, and the median is 84. The longest is “Clear Aligners in Northwest Las Vegas: A Guide for Busy Professionals, Parents, and Teens Near Centennial Hills - SuperSmile Orthodontics”, 137 characters. The three posts I opened credit the author as “admin” in the markup, not Dr. Chamberlain.

05

Some links go through old addresses, and the thank-you page is listed for Google.

The homepage's “Insurances We Take” and “Frequently Asked Questions” links, and the footer's “Cost Estimator” link on all 31 pages, point to old /for-patients/ addresses that redirect to /patient-resources/. Your “Thank you” page is in your sitemap and set to be indexed.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage 82. It found 9 links (the service tiles on the homepage) with no readable name, 8 embedded videos with no title, 3 headings out of order, and page code that turns off zooming on a phone (the viewport tag has user-scalable=0).

What's working

Your phone, 702-645-5100, is a tap-to-call link on all 31 pages I opened, and your address (7090 N. Durango Dr., Ste. 120) and a Map It link are in the header and footer. There is a free-consultation form on the homepage and a Schedule Appointment page that asks for a preferred date and time, and the pages say you respond within one business day. There are separate pages for adult treatment, teens, early orthodontics, clear aligners, braces types, retainers, airway evaluation, whitening, TMJ and 3D imaging, plus insurance, a treatments and fees page, a first-visit page and a patient forms page. Every page has its own title, description, headline and canonical address, the sitemap works, and the homepage did not shift while loading. In my searches, your own name brought up five of your own pages.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the forms work or where they send messages, how fast your office replies, and speed on pages other than the homepage. I opened 31 pages: 28 from your sitemap and three blog posts, and read the titles of all 35 posts. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks /wp-admin/ and a form-upload folder, and names your sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    A working sitemap

    /sitemap_index.xml lists four sitemaps: 28 pages, 35 posts, 2 categories and a locations file. /sitemap.xml and /wp-sitemap.xml redirect to it.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

  • Gap
    Business markup is thin

    The homepage markup has a name, website, email and street address. There is no phone, opening hours, practice type or social links, though the page shows them.

  • Gap
    Opening hours disagree on the page

    “Mon–Fri: 7AM–2PM” and “Mon–Fri: 8AM–5PM” both appear on 20 of the 31 pages I opened. My search for your name answered with 8 AM to 5 PM.

  • Note
    Markup is on only some pages

    The homepage, Contact, About and the three blog posts I opened have markup. The other 25 pages I opened, including every service page and the FAQ page, have none. The three blog posts have FAQ markup. The FAQ page itself does not.

  • Found
    Phone, address and map link are on every page

    702-645-5100 is a call link on all 31 pages I opened, and the footer shows 7090 N. Durango Dr., Ste. 120, Las Vegas, NV 89149 with a Map It link to Google.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
